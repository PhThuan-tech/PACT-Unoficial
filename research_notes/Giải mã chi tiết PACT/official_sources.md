## Q1. Is the supplementary material public, and what does Appendix 6.3 say about alpha_tilde / "post-convergence" detection?

### Takeaway
Yes. The supplementary PDF is public on CVF Open Access. Its Section 6.3 is titled "Windowed stability criterion". It defines a windowed loss-statistics indicator phi and a **hard** gate g = 1[phi <= gamma]. It does **not** give numeric values for W, w1, w2, epsilon or the threshold gamma. It also never writes the formula that combines this gate with alpha_t (from Eq. 14) into alpha_tilde_t. That last step is still unspecified.

- **Section 6.3 verbatim (complete):** "Let {ℓ_i}_{i=1}^{I} denote the training loss sequence. Given a window size W, define the index set Ω_t = {i − W + 1, . . . , i}, the windowed mean and variance:
  - `\bar {\ell }_t=\frac {1}{W}\sum _{k\in \Omega _t}\ell _k,` (40)
  - `v_t=\frac {1}{W-1}\sum _{k\in \Omega _t}\big (\ell _k-\bar {\ell }_t\big )^2,` (41)
  and a simple trend magnitude (finite-difference slope)
  - `m_t=\frac {1}{W}\sum _{k\in \Omega _t}\big (\ell _k-\ell _{k-1}\big ).` (42)
  We quantify (in)stability by a composite indicator
  - `\phi _t = w_1\,\frac {\sqrt {v_t}}{\bar {\ell }_t+\varepsilon } + w_2\,|m_t|,` (43)
  where w1, w2 ≥ 0 and w1 + w2 = 1; and the first term measures fluctuation relative to scale and the second term captures residual drift. The gate is then a hard function:
  - `g_t=\mathbb {I}[\phi _t\le \gamma ],` (44)
  where γ is a stability threshold." — [Supp PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/supplemental/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_CVPR_2026_supplemental.pdf)
- Main-paper text that 6.3 is meant to support (Sec. 3.5): "We then introduce a single adaptive coefficient α̃_t ∈ [0, 1] that jointly gates the plasticity and stability losses. To ensure that these losses are applied only once training has entered a relatively stable regime, α̃_t is further modulated by a measure of training stability and gradually approaches α_t as training converges. This scheduling is motivated by Assumption (3) (bounded per-example loss) ... Consequently, we activate the regularization terms only in this post-convergence phase". Objective: `\mathcal {L}_{\text {total}} \;=\; \mathcal {L}_{\text {cond}} \;+\; \tilde {\alpha }_t \, \big (\mathcal {L}_{\text {marg}} + \mathcal {L}_{\text {pa}}\big )` (15), with footnote 3: "The detailed computation procedure is provided in the Appendix 6.3." Also: "When the model is in an unstable learning phase, α̃_t ≈ 0 and optimization focuses primarily on the conditional loss L_cond." — [Main PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_Constraints_in_Adapter-Based_Continual_Learning_of_Vision-Language_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)
- Proximity weight α_t (main paper, Sec. 3.5): `d_j \;=\; \frac {\|H^{(t)}-H^{(j)}\|_F^2}{\varepsilon + \|H^{(t)}\|_F^2 + \|H^{(j)}\|_F^2}` (12) "where ε = 10^−8"; `j^\star = \arg \min _{j\in \{1,\dots ,t-1\}} d_j` (13); `\alpha _t \;=\; \exp \!\Big (-\frac {d_{j^\star }}{\tau }\Big ), \qquad \tau >0` (14). Here H^(t), H^(j) ∈ R^{d×M} are the responses of the current and historical adapters to the probe bank. The paper gives no numeric value for τ or M — [Main PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_Constraints_in_Adapter-Based_Continual_Learning_of_Vision-Language_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)
- Probe bank (main paper): "We construct a fixed Gaussian probe bank Z = {z_m}_{m=1}^M, with z_m ∼ N(0, I_d), shared across training steps and layers." Footnote 2: "To stabilize scales across layers, one may optionally whiten a running cache of backbone features to unit covariance and resample Z from that whitening map periodically (e.g., every few thousand steps); we found a fixed N(0, I_d) bank sufficient in practice." d = "the adapter input dimension (the width of the residual stream feeding the adapter)" — [Main PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_Constraints_in_Adapter-Based_Continual_Learning_of_Vision-Language_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)

### Inferences
- The most natural reading combining main Eq. 14–15 and supp Eq. 44 is α̃_t = g_t · α_t: a hard 0/1 post-convergence switch times the proximity weight. Neither source writes this, and it conflicts with the main-text phrase "gradually approaches α_t", because a hard indicator cannot do that by itself. "Gradually" may come from α_t changing as the adapter trains, from a ramp the authors did not describe, or from loose wording. Treat this as an inference.
- The subscript t in Eqs. 40–44 is an iteration index ("Ω_t = {i − W + 1, …, i}"), not the task index t used elsewhere. The supp mixes i and t.
- Fig. 6 shows a visible "jump" when PACT switches on. That fits a hard on-switch (Eq. 44) more than a smooth ramp.
- The symbol γ is overloaded. It is the stability threshold in Eq. 44 and also the gate parameter γ_t in g_t = σ(γ_t) (SAI). The g_t in Eq. 44 (stability gate) is also not the same as the g_t in Eq. 45 (convex mixing gate). These are separate quantities with clashing notation.
- From Fig. 6, MTIL runs 3000 iterations per task and PACT activates partway through each task. The activation iteration is not reported.

### Gaps
- No numeric values anywhere for W, w1, w2, ε (in Eq. 43), threshold γ, τ (Eq. 14), or probe bank size M.
- No explicit formula linking g_t (Eq. 44) and α_t (Eq. 14) to α̃_t. It is also unstated whether the gate latches (stays on once triggered) or is re-evaluated every iteration, whether α_t is recomputed every step or once, and whether gradients flow through α_t (d_j depends on the trainable θ_a^t).
- Not stated: which loss sequence ℓ_i feeds the stability criterion (L_cond only, or L_total), or whether per-batch losses are smoothed.
- Not stated: for task t = 1 (no historical adapters), how α_t / j* is defined (argmin over an empty set).

## Q2. What do the appendices say about Q distributions / KL in L_pa, classification heads, gate γ_t init, M, τ, LR, batch size, optimizer, weight decay, iterations, CIL label space, inference, test-time noise?

### Takeaway
The supplement is almost entirely theory (assumptions, PAC-Bayes proof, SAI justification) plus extra experiments. It does **not** give a practical estimator for the KL in L_pa, the form of Q, classifier-head details, LR, batch size, weight decay, γ_t initialization, M, τ, CIL label-space handling, or the inference procedure. It does add three new details: (a) a **layer-wise increasing** convex-mixing schedule with last-layer g_t = 0.5 is used in the main experiments; (b) a reference to an undefined "routing algorithm"; (c) compute cost numbers.

### Cited Findings
**Distribution Q / KL computation**
- Main: "Under deterministic training, the induced distribution over the frozen block θ_a^[t−1] collapses to a Dirac measure at its learned value." Footnote 1: "any outer expectation ... reduces to evaluating the bound at the learned parameters." — [Main PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_Constraints_in_Adapter-Based_Continual_Learning_of_Vision-Language_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)
- Main: `\mathcal {L}_{\text {pa}} \;=\; \text {KL}\!\big (Q_t^a(\theta _a^t)\,\|\,Q_{\text {mlp}}^a(\theta _a^t)\big )` (9), "where Q^a_mlp ≡ Q^a_{t−1} is fixed before observing D_t". There is no practical estimator. Table 5 calls this term "L_stab" — [Main PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_Constraints_in_Adapter-Based_Continual_Learning_of_Vision-Language_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)
- Supp Assumption 2: "in our implementation, the new adapter is initialized from the frozen MLP block via SAI, and P^a_t (the marginal of P_t on θ_a) is instantiated as the corresponding MLP-induced prior, which is fixed before seeing D_t." **Gate γ_t / convex mixing g_t**
- Main SAI (4): "A scalar gate g_t = σ(γ_t), with sigmoid σ and task-specific parameter γ_t, forms a convex combination": `h_{\text {out}} = (1-g_t)\,h_{\text {base}} + g_t\,h_{\text {adapter}}` (8). There is no initialization value for γ_t — [Main PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_Constraints_in_Adapter-Based_Continual_Learning_of_Vision-Language_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)
- Supp SAI proof: "SAI introduces a scalar gate γ_t and sets g_t = σ(γ_t) ∈ (0, 1) ... - **Supp "Analysis of Convex mixing" (new detail):** "In the fixed configuration, a single scalar g_t is shared by all layers ... the layer-wise increasing configuration assigns a depth-dependent coefficient g_t that monotonically increases with depth: we keep g_t close to 0 in shallow layers and gradually increase it towards a larger value in deeper layers. Concretely, we fix the functional form of this schedule and treat the last-layer coefficient as a hyper-parameter, which we sweep from 0 to 1 ... the fixed-g_t setting achieves its best Avg accuracy when g_t = 0.1, reaching about 79.5% ... The optimal configuration is obtained when the last-layer coefficient is g_t = 0.5, where the layer-wise scheme attains an Avg accuracy of 81.05%. We therefore adopt this layer-wise convex mixing strategy with last-layer g_t = 0.5 in our main experiments." — [Supp PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/supplemental/Wang_PACT_Phase-Like_Transition_CVPR_2026_supplemental.pdf)

### Inferences
- The layer-wise g_t schedule (fixed functional form, last-layer value 0.5) suggests g_t may be **fixed/scheduled rather than learned** in the final model. That conflicts with "task-specific parameter γ_t" being trained ("g_t gradually shifts the contribution"). The schedule's functional form (linear? only over layers 3/6/9/12?) is not given.
- Seen-task accuracies stay bit-identical after learning (Table 8) while unseen-task (zero-shot) accuracies change. Together with the unexplained "routing algorithm", this strongly suggests test-time task-identity selection: an oracle task ID, or a router/selector like MoE-Adapters' DDAS used by the cited baselines [66] that sends seen-domain inputs to their own adapter/head and unseen inputs to a zero-shot path. This is inference, not stated.
- "Two lightweight heads" plus "independent classification head" per branch suggests per-task linear (or text-embedding-initialized) heads over the task label space Y_t. The head type (CLIP text-encoder prototypes vs. learned linear) is not specified.
- KL terms with a Dirac/deterministic posterior plus additive 0.1ε activation noise would in practice reduce to a surrogate. Possibilities include an output-space discrepancy (e.g. between adapter and frozen MLP outputs) or parameter-space L2. Which one PACT uses is unknown.

### Gaps
- Not found anywhere: learning rate, batch size, weight decay, LR schedule, warmup, γ_t init, M, τ, W, w1, w2, γ threshold, few-shot (5/16-shot) iteration counts, text prompts/templates, image preprocessing, random seeds.
- Not specified: whether 0.1ε noise is used at test time.
- Not specified: the CIL label space (task-local vs. cumulative head) and how CIL with "100 base classes" (Table 4) maps onto the increments {10, 20, 50}.
- Not specified: the practical KL estimator for L_pa (Eq. 9) and the concrete form of Q (Gaussian or otherwise).
